The problem
Every serious Indian trader runs 4–5 disconnected apps every morning.
"I scan on Chartink, chart on TradingView, check Screener.in for fundamentals, place the order on Kite, then manually log it into a spreadsheet — every single day."
That fragmentation isn't just inconvenient — it makes systematic improvement impossible. When your trade data lives in 5 places, you can never answer the one question that actually matters: why do I keep losing?
Why do I keep losing on breakouts?

AlphaVyuh: AI cross-references your setup tags with P&L automatically.

AlphaVyuh: Trade journal auto-imports from broker with setup tagging.
How do I act fast when I spot a setup?
Now: Spot it in TradingView, scramble to Kite — 60 sec lost.
AlphaVyuh: Click-to-order directly from the chart.
